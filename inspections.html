POINT SYSTEM 1–100. Every team and every individual, every inspection.

  1. Delivery vs deadlines: 30
  2. Verified live: 20
  3. Queue/ticket hygiene: 15
  4. Ownership & check-ins: 15
  5. Rule compliance (lanes, red lines, parallel-work order): 20
  6. No activity in the window: 20

THE CUT 50. Score 50 or higher and you make it. Below 50 and we switch.

  1. Team below 50: switched around — members reassigned, tasks re-scoped, leader replaced if the leader is the problem — until the next inspection shows 50 or higher.
  2. Individual below 50: switched around — new task, new team, or new role — until the next inspection shows 50 or higher.
  3. Leader below 50: stays on as a member, replaced as leader.
  4. Work blocked on Leon's pending decision is not held against the team — logged as waiting-on-Leon, excluded from the cut.
  5. Violations: first occurrence gets called out on the spot; do it again and it counts against the score on the inspection.
  6. Scope: the six teams and their members only. Stonewall is not graded. Viralvideo no longer exists and is not graded.
